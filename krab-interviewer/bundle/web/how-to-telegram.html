💬 Why we need Telegram

Supervisors and dispatch contact drivers through Telegram after you are hired. You must install Telegram and set a public @username before applying.

Install the app

Free on iPhone, Android, and desktop.

@

Set your username

Settings → Username — this is what you enter on the application.

Download Telegram

  • iPhone / iPad: App Store — Telegram Messenger
  • Android: Google Play — Telegram
  • Computer: web.telegram.org

Set your @username (required)

  1. Open Telegram and sign in with your phone number.
  2. Tap Settings (bottom bar on phone, or menu on desktop).
  3. Tap Username and choose a unique name (e.g. johndoe_nj).
  4. Your username appears as @yourname — enter that on the application form.

iPhone quick steps

  1. Install from the App Store link above.
  2. Verify your phone number via SMS.
  3. Set username in Settings before returning to the form.

Android quick steps

  1. Install from Google Play link above.
  2. Sign in with your mobile number.
  3. Open menu → Settings → Username.
